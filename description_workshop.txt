[h1]Many More Mobs[/h1]
Expand the limits of Terraria's world with far more NPCs at once. Many More Mobs can raise the engines hard 200-NPC limit to a configurable total (currently hard-capped at [b]1500[/b]).
The Max NPC budget is split across four categories [b][Town / Boss / Critter / Enemy][/b], letting the screen fill up with an expansive horde of enemies while town NPCs and bosses always have room to spawn.
If you would prefer vanilla 200-limit, a Default mode leaves the regular 200 entity cap untouched, instead despawning low-priority mobs to guarantee important spawns like Town NPCs and Bosses.
[i]Please [b]DO[/b] read the Warnings section[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Raised NPC cap (Expanded Mode)[/b]
[*]     Lift the vanilla 200-NPC limit up to a configurable total (default 750). 
[*]     Town NPCs and Bosses stay in the native low slots, so housing, saving, boss health bars and the map all keep working; enemies and critters fill the expanded slots.

[*][b]Four-category budget[/b]
[*]     Town / Boss / Critter / Enemy, each with its own slot count, so a flood of enemies will never block out town NPC spawns or a boss fight.

[*][b]Default Mode + despawn-to-make-room[/b]
[*]     Keeps the vanilla 200 limit but still get smart prioritization for high spawn-rate playthroughs.
[*]         Low-priority mobs (critters first, then far-off enemies) are despawned to prioritize town NPCs and bosses.

[*][b]Full-size multi-slot Bosses always fit[/b]
[*]    The Destroyer and Eater of Worlds always spawn with every segment, even in a packed world.

[*][b]Spawn controls[/b]
[*]    Global 'spawn rate' and 'max active spawns' multipliers, with optional per biome overrides for fine-grained pacing instead.

[*][b]Spawn-item tuning[/b]
[*]    Config page to amplify, weaken or disable every spawn-affecting item (Water Candle, Battle Potion, Peace Candle, Calming Potion etc...).

[*][b]Event scaling[/b]
[*]    Lengthen invasions and Moon Events so they don't become trivially short at high spawn rates.
[*]    Old One's Army spawn-rate and wave-length controls.

[*][b]Horde combat assists[/b]
[*]    Per damage class (Melee / Ranged / Magic / Summon): no pierce-falloff (piercing shots and whips keep full damage across a crowd instead of decaying to nothing), extra pierce, anti-tunnel (fast projectiles hit every enemy along their path), and "melee hits all in swing". The ones that fix behaviour vanilla balanced around a handful of enemies are ON by default, the rest are opt-in.
[/list]


[h2]Config Explanations[/h2]
[b]Mode: Default vs Expanded[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Default[/b] keeps Terraria's 200-NPC limit and does [b]no engine patching[/b] - 
the safest, most compatible choice. 
[*]     The category caps become [b]ceilings[/b] within the shared 200, and the mod despawns low-priority mobs so Town and Boss spawns always succeed.

[*][b]Expanded[/b] raises the limit to [b]Max Total NPCs[/b]. Here the four category caps are a hard [b]reservation[/b] and should, for the best experience, sum to the value of Max Total NPCs (default 40 + 160 + 25 + 525 = 750). 
[*]     Town and Boss live in the native 0-199 slots; Critter and Enemy live in the expanded 200+ slots.

[*]Changing Mode, Max Total, or the caps requires a mod reload.
[/list]


[h2]Expanded Slots Explained[/h2]
[i]Only relevant if you're playing with Expanded Mode [b]ON[/b][/i].
Picture the NPC list as two stretches: the [b]Default[/b] engine slots (0-199) and the [b]Expanded[/b] slots stacked on top (up to your Max Total NPCs).
[list]
[*][b]Town NPCs and Bosses[/b] fill from the bottom up, so they settle into the Default 0-199 slots. That's the whole trick to keeping housing, saving, boss health bars and the map working like vanilla - which is why their caps only go up to 200 combined.
[*][b]Critters and Enemies[/b] fill from the top down, so they live up in the Expanded slots.
[/list]

The four caps decide how that budget gets split:
[list]
[*]Ideally they add up to your Max Total NPCs (the defaults already do: 40 + 160 + 25 + 525 = 750).
[*]If they add up to [b]LESS[/b], the leftover slots simply sit empty - totally fine.
[*]If they add up to [b]MORE[/b], the caps scale down evenly so everything still fits under the cap.
[*]The zones aren't hard walls, if the Expanded slots ever completely fill, enemies fall back into any spare Default slots instead of refusing to spawn.
[*][b]One catch[/b] - if you DO overflow the Expanded slots (Enemy + Critter set 
 combined value equals higher than; Max Total NPCs - 200), the enemies spilling down into the low slots can crowd out Town NPC / Boss spawns.
[*]     The despawn-to-make-room safety net doesn't yet tell low- from high-slots in Expanded, so recommend keeping Enemy + Critter caps within the Expanded space (the defaults already do) and you're golden. 
[*]         For the best Expanded experience; MaxNPCTotal = ((TownNPCCap + BossCap equal 200) + (CritterCap + EnemyCap = (MaxNPCTotal - 200))). A proper fix is on the way.
[/list]
[b]Want a bigger horde?[/b] Raise Max Total NPCs [b]and[/b] the Enemy cap together, bumping the total alone doesn't do much, since the Enemy cap is what actually decides how many enemies the world holds.


[h2]Warnings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]SINGLE-PLAYER ONLY[/b] - the cap raise has no multiplayer sync yet and can desync or crash in MP. Multiplayer support is planned for a later version.

[*][b]EXPERIMENTAL CAP RAISE[/b] - Expanded mode patches the engine at load. Default mode does no patching if you want to play it safe.
[*]     If running Expanded mode, test on a [b]backup world[/b] first or preferably create a new world + character for playthrough.

[*][b]PERFORMANCE[/b] scales with NPC count - very high caps plus high spawn rates will most likely lower FPS.
[*]     From testing there, a known limitation here is excessive amounts of projectiles from high enemy count leads to slowdown.

[*][b]MOD COMPATIBILITY[/b] - other mods that also add lots of NPCs or touch the spawn system may interact unpredictably with Expanded mode.
[*]     Some light cross-mod testing has been done with less intrusive modes like [Smarth Regrowth], [Autofish], and [Cheat Sheet].
[*]     Safe assumption is any mod adding Town NPCs or Enemies will most likely not work as expected.
[/list]


[h2]Known Bugs and Limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Multiplayer is not supported [i]yet[/i] (ref Warnings above).
[*]Config sliders snap and jank with wide ranges, a limitation of tModLoader's default config screen. A nicer custom config UI is a dream for later.
[*]Expanded mode only: if you push Enemy/Critter caps past what the Expanded slots can hold (Max Total - 200), the overflow can block Town NPC / Boss spawns - despawn-to-make-room doesn't tell low from high slots in Expanded yet. Keep the four caps within Max Total NPCs (the defaults do). Fix coming.
[*]Enemies in the expanded slots don't yet have custom map icons / bestiary polish.
[*]The Wyvern will mostly spawn at reduced length in a fully packed world.
[*]On max spawn rate, noticeable churn/slowdown during Lunar Pillars event due to amount of projectiles from enemies.
[/list]
This list will grow and shrink as playtesters report issues - thank you for the feedback!
